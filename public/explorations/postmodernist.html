Reversible visual exploration

Postmodernist: playful layers and contrast

Reference

Super lamp prototype, designed by Martine Bedin for Memphis and made by Fausto Celati, 1981, Italy; V&A Museum no. M.1-2011.

View the V&A reference and image ↗

Design choices

  • Layer offset color panels around the unchanged white tee.
  • Repeat small circles to echo the lamp's playful light points.
  • Mix visual cues while keeping the same copy clear and readable.

Audience effect and risk

Hypothesis: expressive framing may help the plain tee feel personal. Risk: the surrounding layers may feel too loud for someone who avoids bold statements.
