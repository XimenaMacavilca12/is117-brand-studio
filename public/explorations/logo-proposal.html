Consistency wordmark

A one-color serif wordmark with repeated, offset blocks as a small postmodern accent. This is a proposal, not a final or trademark-cleared logo.

Review points

  • Silhouette: three repeated blocks lead into the wordmark.
  • Readability: the name remains written out at header size.
  • Contrast: dark ink sits on the light paper sample.
  • Distinctiveness: no clearance or uniqueness search performed.
