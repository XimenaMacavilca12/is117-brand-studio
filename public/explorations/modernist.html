Modernist: function in a clear grid

Reference

Dell-Lampe Type K, desk lamp designed by Christian Dell and made by Zimmermann GmbH, 1929, Germany; V&A Museum no. M.27-1992.

View the V&A reference and image ↗

Design choices

  • Align the copy, product, and small labels to a measured grid.
  • Use clear type scale and rules to make the next step easy to scan.
  • Keep the palette restrained and the product image unaltered.

Audience effect and risk

Hypothesis: the orderly layout may make a plain tee feel like a deliberate everyday choice. Risk: it may feel too clinical or anonymous to someone seeking more expression.
