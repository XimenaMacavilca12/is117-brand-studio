06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Relaxed, approachable, and understated. The examples below show three honest ways to welcome the intended visitor.

Liking / Familiar moments

“For coffee catch-ups, dates, and regular days—keep it simple, keep it you.”

Uses the learner's real audience examples; it does not claim that other customers agree.

Reciprocity / A useful free tip

“For coffee or a date, try a muted-color coat, jacket, or zip-up. A simple layer can keep the shirt from distracting and let the conversation be about you.”

A no-pressure styling suggestion is offered without requiring a purchase or sign-up.

Brand story / Wearer-first

We believe the person wearing the shirt should stand out, not the shirt. A simple T-shirt gives you room to express who you are without distraction.

States the learner's brand idea without adding product claims.

A living reference. The storefront and this guide use the same colors, typography, logo, and components. The styling suggestion is an idea, not a product claim or guarantee.
